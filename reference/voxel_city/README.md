# Voxel World

A fully procedural, infinitely streamable voxel world at **12.5 cm resolution**,
focused on a modern city you can walk through: every building is enterable,
every room is reachable through real doors and stairs, and the city has
verticality above and below the streets (elevated highways, skybridges between
office buildings, parking garages with ramps, a subway, a walkable sewer network, basements, and underground
complexes out in the countryside). Beyond the cities lies realistically scaled
nature: mountain ranges up to ~6 km with forests fading into rock and snow,
desert mesas, dunes and canyons, forest ravines, rivers, lakes and caves.
Black Mesa-style research complexes, military bases and hollowed-out mountain
strongholds reach deep underground and are linked by deep service tunnels.
Climate-driven biomes (temperate and boreal forest, grassland, tundra, desert,
savanna, rainforest, wetland) cover the land, and the same world can be one
face of a cube-sphere planet, a finite world that wraps around (a flat
planet) or a single island in an endless sea. Every world can be shown in
spring, summer, autumn or winter. Towns grow organically: old centres of
irregular cobbled blocks and lanes, districts decided block by block, edges
that fray into meadows and woods, and landmarks (a market square, the
church, a cemetery, allotments, a wharf). Presets bundle these into ready
worlds, among them a bleak Nordic island and small 4-6 km islands with one
old northern town (a Bergen-like harbour town and a White Sea town among
them).

```bash
npm install
npm run dev      # viewer at http://localhost:5173 (add -- --port N if taken)
npm test         # determinism, seams, roads, interiors, walkability, underground, nature, verticality, sites, seasons, towns
npm run lint
npm run build
```

```bash
node scripts/golden.js --check   # every preset still generates bit for bit what it did before the angled world (test/golden/presets.json)
node scripts/render-map.js --preset angledCities --size 1600      # the angled world: diagonal boulevards, tilted old-town lanes

node scripts/render-iso.js --preset angledCities --x 490 --y 150 --size 90    # buildings turned to face a tilted street
node scripts/render-iso.js --preset angledNordicTown --variant forest --x -1100 --y -1100 --size 64   # a wild boreal wood
node scripts/render-iso.js --preset angledOldHarbourTown --x 420 --y -364 --size 36 --parts 1   # a 12.5% street as one pitched plane (parts in their own lattices)
node scripts/render-slice.js --preset angledOldHarbourTown --axis y --x 420.06 --y -364 --width 40 --bottom 34 --top 46 --step 0.25 --parts 1   # its section: plane vs the grid's steps (drop --parts)
node scripts/render-iso.js --preset angledCities --x -645 --y -695 --size 14 --yaw 225 --parts 1   # a corner bay at 43.6° on a building square to the grid (--wings 0: without)
node scripts/golden.js --angled --check                                  # the angled presets against their record (--write after a deliberate change)
```

Every script takes `--preset id [--variant id] [--season spring|summer|autumn|winter] [--seed n]`.

## What gets generated

| Layer | Content |
| --- | --- |
| World modes | an endless plane; a **wrapping world** (`chart: "torus"`, `size`): the plane repeats every `size` m east-west and north-south, every field and lattice is periodic (walk far enough and you are back in the same street), climate runs from an equator to a pole and back; an **island** (`mode: "island"`): one island of a given size in an endless sea, fully mapped by the biome system, with its own towns, villages and hamlets; one face of a cube-sphere planet |
| Presets | `config/presets.js`: cities in countryside, infinite city, wrapping world (48 / 96 / 192 km), island and Nordic island (small / medium / large), the **Nordic small-town island** (skerry / fjord / forest variants, 4-5 km), the **old harbour town** (Bergen-like, 6 km, steep, wharf) and the **White Sea town** (flat Karelian forest and bog island with a wooden town and Soviet blocks), planet faces; each preset is config overrides, a default season and a viewer mood per season (sky, fog, a low winter sun, pale summer days) |
| Macro fields | settlements (cities in countryside, `infiniteCity`, or an island's sited places), downtown-ness, climate (temperature with latitude on planets and wrapping worlds, moisture; presets can pin the climate), style noise |
| Terrain | a stack of registered landforms: continental swell, hills, mountain belts and massifs (ridged relief up to ~6 km with arêtes and couloirs, broad foothills, U-shaped glacial trunk valleys, realistic slopes), raised dry plateaus with mesas, dunes and ledged canyons (with a stream or a dry wash on the floor), forest ravines with creeks; graded city land; sea level water; walking-scale relief in open country (knolls, hollows, hummocks, tree-throw mounds) growing with ruggedness, so glaciated northern country (and the Nordic islands) is knobbly and mild lowlands gentler; mountain flanks furrowed by couloirs and gullies down the fall line; canyon walls in bands of benches, scree aprons and cliffs, with buttresses, alcoves and side gullies; granite outcrops cracked by joints into slabs and ledges. The spawn city always sees a range 10-35 km away. Close up, mountain ground has hummocks, knolls and crags, bedded rock strata in cliffs and cuts, boulder fields above the tree line, and wind-scoured snow with rock outcrops; the terrain is continuous at voxel scale (no seams or walls from the landform stack) |
| Road network | global arterial grid (avenues on the subway lines and through the centre, two-lane main roads between them), collectors that cross, jog or stop at a T (not every cell is four squares), grid streets with varied block widths and jogs, subdivided and organic local streets, cobbled lanes, alleys, pedestrian streets, wobbly rural roads; junctions blend into hilly roads over a length that keeps the grade gentle |
| Historic cores | `flavor.oldCore`: every city of the modern, historic, nordic and desert flavors grows an old core round its centre (the `oldcore` district: small irregular blocks on cobbled streets and lanes, squares, a church, rendered and brick houses with pitched roofs, a medina in the desert), laid out block by block wherever the core reaches |
| Road levels | streets are built, not draped: every road has a balanced cut-and-fill profile (half cutting, half embankment where it crosses a bump or climbs a scarp), grade-limited per class (8% arterials to 16% lanes) and steeper where the land demands it (hill towns have steep streets), level across its width; a side street meets the through street at its level, junction levels blend continuously (close junctions share the rise between them); the ground is cut and filled into embankments beside roads in open country and city blocks are stretched between their sidewalk levels |
| Site grading | `city/grading.js`: every building stands on a level pad at its entrance level (footprint, a 1.5 m apron, a civic forecourt); gardens, yards, courtyards, parks and leftover ground ease from the pads back to the block surface, so on a hillside houses sit on terraces with sloping gardens and neighbours meet without cliffs (retaining walls only where a slope has no room); car parks, work yards, schoolyards and wharves stay level terraces; lots keep off every street's right-of-way (no street through a building); trees, plants and props stand on the ground as the tiles shape it |
| Road surface | SDF carriageways with exact corner fillets, curbs, sidewalks with joints, tree pits / grass strips, lane lines, double yellow, parking lanes & stall ticks, medians (raised + hatched), crosswalks with lowered tactile curbs, stop lines and lane arrows, manholes, drains; narrow village streets; country roads with shoulders and delineator posts |
| Settlements | towns and cities on a 9 km lattice plus villages and hamlets on a 3.6 km lattice between them (their own main street and cross street, houses thinning out towards the fields), farms (farmhouse, barn with stalls and hay, silos, bales, fences) along the country roads |
| Districts | downtown, midtown, mixed, residential, suburban, industrial, heavy industry (each town has an industrial quarter on one side of its outer ring), bleak housing projects on its fringe, microdistricts, port, village, park, rural (+ military / research / stronghold for sites); every block takes the district of its own place in the town (not the whole ~300 m sub-cell), so quarters meet along ragged lines |
| Town shape | towns have lobes (a noise at their own size) and a wide rim over which they thin out: on the rim a block is built only where the town reaches (a fringe noise decides), local streets are built only where they serve a kept block and are trimmed to it, sub-cells the town only reaches into are laid out too, and houses drop out lot by lot; fields, meadows and woods reach in between |
| Old towns | the **organic** street pattern splits blocks at irregular places (T-junctions, jogged crossings, blocks of every size) with narrow cobbled **lanes** between some of them; **adaptive** flavors grow the whole town that way, each part at the block size of its own district (small blocks in the old core, big yards by the works); cobbled streets with dark gutters and flagstone pavements, iron lanterns, gently bending narrow main streets, no collector grid; wooden houses stand a little back or forward and leave passages to the back yards (cobbled or gravel courts, kitchen gardens, sheds, woodpiles, fences), rendered blocks get gabled or hipped roofs |
| Landmarks | `city/townPlan.js` places each town's landmarks once: a cobbled **market square** (monument, market stalls, lanterns, trees) near the centre and towards the harbour, the **main church** on the highest ground nearby, a walled **cemetery** with a chapel, rows of graves and an avenue of trees on the quiet edge of town, parks, schools, a sports ground, **allotment gardens** (plots, huts, beds, fruit trees) on the fringe, a garage cooperative and a **wasteland** of rubble and scrub by the works, and in harbour towns a **wharf** of narrow gabled warehouses (Bryggen); each lands on the nearest block that suits it |
| Blocks & lots | perimeter rows with corner lots, downtown parcels, suburban lots, industrial yards, plazas, parks, gardens, parking lots, sports courts, tank farms (bunded storage tanks, pipe racks, distillation columns, flare stack), container yards (stacks, lanes, gantry cranes) |
| Buildings | houses, row houses (stoops), walk-ups, mid-rises, offices, towers (podium + setbacks), parking garages (open decks + stacked ramps), schools (classrooms, gym, cafeteria, library, schoolyard), warehouses, factories, farmsteads; Nordic wooden town houses (2-3 floors under a steep gable, shops below in an old town), forest cabins (log or falu-red boards on a stone plinth, porch, living room, kitchen, bedrooms, bath) and wooden long churches with a belfry tower and spire in a walled churchyard of headstones and grave crosses (in Russian and Karelian towns Orthodox churches: onion domes of gilt, green, blue or aspen shingle on the bell tower or a tented spire and along the nave, three-bar crosses, an icon screen inside); wharf warehouses (three or four storeys, gable to the street); cemetery chapels |
| Civic buildings | `buildings/civic.js`: every town gets its public buildings, venues and big shops on lots of their own (`city/townPlan.js` places them by town size and flavor): a **town hall** by the square (portico, council chamber, registry), **museums** (galleries of display cases, vitrines, skeletons, sculpture) and **art galleries** small and large (white rooms hung with paintings), **concert halls** (double-height auditorium with rows of seats and a stage, foyer bar on the balcony level, dressing rooms, a lit marquee) and, in Russian and Karelian towns, a **house of culture** (auditorium, dance hall, club rooms), **cinemas** (two or three screens, a kiosk), **music clubs** (a stage, a bar, a neon sign), **libraries**, **hotels** (reception, restaurant, guest rooms), **department stores** (a Soviet *univermag* in Russian towns), **supermarkets** (car park, checkouts, aisles, fridges, produce, a back store and loading dock), **market halls** (gabled, stalls of fish, bread, produce) by a harbour, **petrol stations** (a canopy over pump islands, a price pylon, a shop), **police stations** (front desk, interview rooms, holding cells, briefing room, evidence store, a garage), **fire stations** (red engine bays with fire trucks and hose racks, dorms and a kitchen upstairs) and **hospitals** (emergency entrance with an ambulance porch, x-ray, operating theatres, wards with curtained beds) or, in smaller and Russian towns, **polyclinics** (doctors' rooms and waiting corners on every floor); classical stone fronts with porticos, Stalinist, panel, wooden or glass fronts by flavor; signs by kind (fascia, marquee, neon, red cross, police band, name plate, banners) |
| Interiors | apartments (band / through / railroad / studio layouts), corridors & cores, lobbies, shops with backrooms (bakeries, butchers, fishmongers, pharmacies with a green neon cross, bookshops, clothes shops, florists, hardware stores, barbers, pubs, banks, launderettes, grocers, galleries, souvenir shops, a *produkty* in Russian towns; each with its own sign over the door), open-plan offices, meeting rooms, restrooms, parking basements, storage basements, warehouse annexes with mezzanines; warehouses hold pallet racks, or are distribution centres (conveyors), self-storage units, cold stores or timber merchants |
| Vertical circulation | U-stairs (1-voxel risers, 2-voxel treads) from the lowest basement to a roof bulkhead, elevators with shafts |
| Facades | brick / plaster / concrete / glass curtain / deco / futurist / siding / industrial styles, painted board cladding (falu red, ochre, white, grey-blue, green, with board seams and corner boards), northern render, tarred logs, recessed windows on a shared bay grid, storefronts, awnings, canopies, cornices, parapets |
| Furniture | ~40 prefabs placed per room type with door clearance and a walkability guarantee |
| Street dressing | lamps, traffic signals, hydrants, benches, bins, bus shelters, street trees (one species and age class per street: limes, maples, oaks, cherries, birches; some streets without), hedges, fences, playgrounds; landscape parks (`city/parks.js`: winding walks from the entrances to a hub with a fountain, an irregular pond with reeds and willows, groves of one species each, solitary trees on open lawns, unmown meadow, benches and lamps along the walks); plazas of large slabs with rows of trees in beds; parked cars and vehicles are optional (`vehicles.parked`, a viewer checkbox; off by default: traffic will be simulated) |
| Highways | an intercity network linking neighbouring settlements plus urban links between highway nodes, never a spur or a dead end in the air (a route ending at a town comes down to grade); grade-limited (5%) deck profile that stays elevated over cities, roads and rivers and runs near grade in open country on embankments, through cuttings with retaining walls and through lit tunnels under hills; level junction plateaus where three or four highways meet (no barrier across, a railing round the rim); interchanges where a highway crosses an arterial: ramps within 7% that land in the arterial's carriageway at its level and meet the deck tangent to it, through an opening in the barrier, left out where a street below would lack headroom; piers every 32 m that are never left out (moved off a street, or a portal straddling it); corridors kept free of buildings, trees and props; the lanes, ramps and signals are in the structvox road export |
| Subway | lines under arterials, stations with island platforms, mezzanines, street entrances, transfer stations between lines; the running tracks spread to the platform tracks over the last 40 m (the tunnel widening with them), sleepers in phase |
| Sewers | vaulted brick tunnels 5 m under the streets of dense districts with channels and walkways, junction chambers with ladder shafts to manholes (some open, fenced by cones), pillared overflow halls with a basin and a stair to the sidewalk |
| Islands | small islands (4-6 km) scale everything down: a town of a few thousand, tarns instead of lakes, a finer road lattice, two-lane main streets, a town that climbs the hillside from its harbour (`island.townRise`); a lighthouse on the headland nearest the harbour, rows of boathouses (naust) at the water near every place, fish-drying racks by the hamlets, cairns on the fell tops (`world/landmarks.js`); a warped, elongated coast with bays, headlands and points; fjords winding into the highlands; fells and mountains on one side plunging into the sea; sea cliffs (10-55 m, rock with strata) where the coast is rocky, beaches (sand, or shingle in the north) where it is low; a shelf with skerries (rock islets); the main town on a sheltered stretch of coast at the origin, graded down to a waterfront 2.5 m above the sea, with a small harbour (quays, cranes, a moored freighter); smaller towns, villages and hamlets on the lowlands, some on the shore, some in the forest, joined by country trunk roads routed around the sea and steep ground; cabins in the woods and along the shores; nothing is planned over the water |
| Seasons | `world.season` (`world/season.js`), keyed on the local temperature (climate cooled by altitude), so warm lands barely change: **winter** snow on open ground, yards, parks, forest floors and roofs (bare patches where it is thin), snow on conifer tiers, bare broadleaves, frozen lakes and rivers (the sea stays open); **spring** fresh grass, young light-green leaves, blossom, late snow up high and in the far north; **summer** wild flowers in the meadows (lupins, buttercups, daisies, campion, heather in bloom); **autumn** golden birches, orange oaks, red rowans, straw-coloured grass, leaf litter, red dwarf shrubs on the fells, some trees already bare, the first snow on the tops; distant forest canopies change colour too. A climate's explicit `snowCover` / `freeze` still pins snow and ice |
| Rivers & lakes | meandering rivers with springs and varying widths, valleys with sand banks in the countryside, stone quays with railings and riverside promenades in cities, bridges (deck, piers, railings) wherever a road crosses; lowland and alpine lakes with irregular shores whose level sits below the rim, and big lakes kilometres across; half of those lie against a town, whose waterfront is graded down to a harbour: straight quays, container terminals with ship-to-shore cranes and moored cargo ships, warehouses and tank farms; water freezes (with snow drifts) where it is as cold as the snow line |
| Caves | worm tunnels and caverns under the countryside with mossy mouths, gravel floors, stalagmites, stalactites and crystals |
| Nature | climate biomes (temperate, grassland, boreal, tundra, desert, savanna, rainforest, wetland with ponds, northern bog), altitude lapse rate with a tree line, alpine meadows, scree, cliffs and a snow line, striped strata in deserts, farmland, trees built from a model per tree (`nature/trees.js`: a tapering, leaning trunk with a flared foot, limbs and twigs, foliage in lumpy clusters with satellites, shaded sunlit / body / shadow): oak (broad, low fork), maple / beech (tall oval), lime / plane (street trees), birch (white stems, often two or three, airy crowns), rowan, cherry (flat, spreading), weeping willow (hanging curtains), poplar (a column), Scots pine (long bare trunk, orange above, tufts of needles), Norway spruce (drooping whorls in irregular lobes), krummholz and stunted mountain birch near the tree line, juniper, acacia, jungle, palm, cactus, shrubs; forests grow in stands (each species follows its own noise: spruce stands, birch groves, pines on the rocks), clumps and gaps, crowded trees tall and slim and open-grown ones broad, young trees in gaps and at edges, dead snags; an understory up close (ferns, blueberry, shrubs, junipers, saplings, fallen mossy logs, stumps) and a fringe of shrubs and saplings at forest edges; ground cover up close (`nature/groundcover.js`: tufts of tall grass in drifts, wild flowers as plants, reeds in clumps along the water); ground materials mix in dithered drifts (moss, needle litter and lichen under conifers, leaf litter and moss under broadleaves); meadow glades in the woods; thick woods (a second, younger tree under the canopy in the densest stands, thickets of young growth, more saplings, deadwood, ferns and junipers; aspens, alders, larches in the dry taiga, hazels at the woodland edge); the forest floor up close with blueberry and lingonberry carpets, moss cushions, herbs, heather, stones, twigs and the odd mushroom; mossy glacial erratics through rugged woods and heath; farmland (`nature/farmland.js`): fields of varying size round every farmstead and across mild farming country, crops by climate and season (wheat golden in summer and stubble in autumn, rapeseed flowering in spring, potatoes, maize, mown hay, pasture, fallow; hay, pasture and potato plots in the north), grass margins, hedgerows and, in the north, low walls of field stones; willow, birch and poplar belts along rivers, lakes and creeks; tree-lined avenues on some country roads near towns and farms; no trees on scarps and embankment faces, pines on dry steep slopes; glacially smoothed granite outcrops with lichen breaking through forest and meadow, bare rock slabs on low island shores; creeks winding through wet country in shallow valleys with sloping banks (never below the sea); distant forests become a canopy of crowns that follows the same stands (pointed spruce crowns, rounded broadleaf ones, a leafy forest edge); city street trees follow the climate (birches, rowans and spruces up north) |
| Skybridges | glass walkways between facing office buildings at podium level (many in futuristic cities), with doors carved into both floor plans |
| Cities | flavors re-weight styles, archetypes and floors (and can re-map districts, also by place in the town) per settlement: modern, historic, futuristic, Soviet (Stalinist centre, microdistricts of prefab panel slabs and point towers in shared courtyards with playgrounds and lock-up garages), desert (adobe) and nordic towns chosen by climate; island harbour towns; the **bleak northern harbour town** of the Nordic island: an old centre of wooden and rendered town houses with steep roofs on narrow lanes, a ring of post-war Stalinist and brick blocks, panel-block microdistricts in some quarters and wooden houses in others, an industrial quarter with its housing projects, a small harbour; villages keep their region's look at village height |
| Sites | **military base**: fenced compound, gate, watchtowers, radar, hangars/HQ/barracks with full interiors, bunker with stairs down to a multi-level Doom-like complex. **Research complex** (Black Mesa style): campus with offices, hangar and parking garage, radomes and dishes, portal blocks down to four themed sectors (labs, containment cells, power / reactor, barracks) on 2-3 staggered levels with atriums, test chambers, octagonal and round rooms, keycard door frames, joined by a tram loop. **Mountain stronghold**: a blast-door portal on a mountain flank, reached by a service road that winds down the flank along the contours (cut and filled into the slope, 9% grade), a vehicle tunnel into a vaulted cavern with office blocks and a hangar (full interiors), trucks and tanks, stairs down to a two-sector complex with its own tram. Every room of every level is reachable (tested). **Link tunnels**: deep service-road tunnels join neighbouring sites' tram stations / deepest levels, grade-limited and always under rock |

## Viewer

The HUD (top left) holds the camera modes, the map, the voxel inspector and
the panel; under it a readout (position, frame rate, tiles queued) and
status chips (waiting for the ground to stream in, a lost GPU context, failed
tiles, changed world settings). The panel has four tabs and fits any window:
a side panel that scrolls, a sheet along the bottom on narrow or low screens.

- **Orbit** (1) – drag to rotate, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, WASD pans
- **Fly** (2) – click to capture the mouse (a finger drags to look), WASD + Q/E, Shift = fast, wheel = speed
- **Walk** (3) – first person with gravity and voxel collision, climbs stairs and curbs, climbs ladders (look up + W, S to descend, Space lets go), Space jumps. Entering it puts the walker on the ground under the view; a walker put inside a wall steps out, one that falls out of what has streamed in is put back on the ground
- **World** tab – preset, size / variant, seed (or a random one), season, and what is generated: highways, subway, rivers, lakes, caves, parked vehicles; in the angled presets each angled feature and how parts are drawn (in their own lattices, as structvox will, or stepped into the world grid). Changes wait for **Apply & regenerate**
- **View** tab – time of day (dawn, noon, dusk, night), detail (LOD factor), view distance, resolution, shadows, fog, field of view, the cutaway (hide everything above a height, or above the camera), the walker's speed and noclip
- **Debug** tab – tint tiles by LOD or the angled world's parts, tile borders, wireframe, freeze streaming (look at LOD boundaries from outside); frame rate, draw calls, triangles, geometries, the far plane, the GPU context; tiles shown / ready / queued / loading per LOD, generation time per tile, failed tiles with the last error; the camera and walker; copy all of it as JSON
- **Inspect** (I) – click a voxel (orbit) or press I at the crosshair (fly, walk): its material in the world grid and in any part there (with the part's local cell), the building, road, highway, district, cell and settlement
- **Places** tab – jump to downtown, a subway platform, the sewer hall or an open manhole, a river bridge, a skybridge, the highway deck and an interchange, a park, a suburban house, the civic buildings, other cities by flavor, a village, a farm, a harbour, housing projects, the sites and a deep link tunnel; in the angled presets one of each kind of part (a turned building, a row, a corner bay, a canted bay, a chamfer, a wing, a pitched street, a garage ramp). **Bookmarks** keep views per world in the browser; **Copy link** (K) puts the world and the view in a URL (`?preset=angledCities&seed=1337&at=x,y,z,yaw,pitch,mode`); **Go to** takes metres
- **Map** (M) – vector plan of districts, lots, buildings, highways, subway, sewers, sites and link tunnels; click a building to inspect every floor (ramps shown as chevrons), double-click to teleport; the **World** layer shows biomes, relief, cities, highways and sites at regional scale
- **Keys** – 1 2 3 modes · M map · P panel · I inspect · L tint · B tile borders · F freeze streaming · N noclip · G back on the ground · K copy link

The URL keeps the world (`?preset=nordicTown&variant=fjord&season=autumn&seed=7&off=caves&parts=grid`); on a wrapping world the readout shows the position on the world and the laps walked.
